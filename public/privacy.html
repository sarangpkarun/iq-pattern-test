Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data ("we") collects through this website, why, who else handles it, and your choices. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and keep the collection small.

1. What we collect

  • Email address – only if you decide to unlock your report.
  • Your test answers, how long the test took, and your age range (if you choose one) – to calculate your result.
  • Payment status – whether your payment succeeded, and Razorpay’s order and payment reference numbers.
  • Technical and advertising data – such as pages visited, device and browser type, and cookie identifiers set by Meta (see section 4).
  • IP address and browser type – saved with your order when you pay, so our advertising measurement can match the purchase to the ad that led to it (see section 4).

We do not collect or store your card, UPI or bank details. Those go directly to Razorpay.

2. Why we use it

  • To score your test, show your result and email it to you.
  • To process your payment and prevent fraud or duplicate charges.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and the test itself, for example by recalibrating scores using anonymous answer patterns.

3. Who handles your data

  • Razorpay – payment processing. Your email and answers are saved with your Razorpay order record, because we do not run our own database.
  • Resend – sends your result email.
  • Vercel – hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) – advertising measurement, see below.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services above, and where the law requires.

4. Cookies, Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We use the Meta Pixel in your browser and Meta’s Conversions API from our server to see which ads lead to completed tests and purchases. This uses cookies and similar identifiers, and when you buy, a one-way hashed version of your email address and the purchase details are sent to Meta. You can control ad cookies in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences.

Your browser also stores your test progress and, after payment, your payment reference in local storage on your own device so a refresh does not lose your result. You can clear this by clearing site data or using “Retake the test”.

5. How long we keep it

Order records, including your email and answers, are kept for as long as needed to support you and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, then deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see section 6), except where we must keep payment records by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent to marketing or advertising use. Email from the address you used and we will respond . If we cannot resolve your concern you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

This service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the site, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS, secrets are kept on the server, and payments are handled by Razorpay. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
